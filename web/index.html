PrusaSlicer → OrcaSlicer,
in your browser.

Drop a PrusaSlicer config bundle and get back an .orca_printer file ready to import. Nothing to install; your file stays on your computer.

Your files never leave your computer. This page runs convert.py — the exact same converter the desktop app uses — compiled to WebAssembly and executed locally. There is no server, no upload and no analytics. Open your browser's network tab and check.
